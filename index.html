CSV / Manual Import

Paste or upload a CSV of phone numbers and names, or add one-off recipients by hand. Up to 100 per batch.

Template Composer

Personalize with {name}-style tags, format with bold/italic/strikethrough/mono, and preview exactly what each recipient sees.

Image & Video Attachments

Upload a file directly or paste a media link — sent through WhatsApp's normal photo/video flow, or as an uncompressed document.

🛡️

Safety Guardrails

Randomized delays, jitter, and an hourly send cap keep your account's sending pattern from looking automated.

📡

Live Queue Monitor

Watch each recipient move through pending → sending → sent/failed in real time, right in the side panel.

🔒

Runs Through Your Own Session

No third-party server in the loop — everything happens inside your browser, against your own logged-in WhatsApp Web.
