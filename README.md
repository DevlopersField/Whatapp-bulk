## 🌟 What Does This Tool Do?

Normally, sending a message to 100 or 1,000 customers on WhatsApp requires copying, pasting, and sending one by one for hours.

This Chrome Extension does all that work for you automatically:
1. It opens each customer's chat on [WhatsApp Web](https://web.whatsapp.com).
2. It types a personalized message with their real name (e.g., *"Hi John, your bill is ready"*).
3. It attaches your PDF, photo, or document.
4. It sends the message and waits a few natural seconds before moving to the next person—just like a real human would!

---

## 🚀 How to Use It (4 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Open WhatsApp Web
1. Open Google Chrome on your computer.
2. Go to **[web.whatsapp.com](https://web.whatsapp.com)**.
3. Open WhatsApp on your phone, tap **Linked Devices**, and scan the QR code to log in.

### Step 2: Open the Extension & Add Contacts
1. Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar (top right).
2. You can add contacts in any of these 3 easy ways:
   * **Upload CSV File**: Upload an Excel/CSV file with columns like `phone,name`.
   * **Paste Text**: Copy and paste your list directly into the box.
   * **Single Number**: Quickly add one phone number at a time.
   * *(You can add unlimited contacts—there is no limit!)*

### Step 3: Write Your Message & Attach Files
1. Type your message in the box.
2. Use `{name}` anywhere in your message to automatically insert the customer's name!
3. Format text with **Bold**, *Italic*, or add links with one click.
4. *(Optional)* Click **Upload File** to attach a PDF document, invoice, product photo, or video.

### Step 4: Test & Start
1. Under **Send Test To**, enter your own phone number and click **Send Test** to see how it looks on your phone.
2. If everything looks good, click **Start Processing**!
3. Sit back and relax while the extension delivers your messages.

## 💡 Key Features Explained in Plain English

### 1. Unlimited Contacts (`∞`)
There is no cap or restriction. Whether you have 10 people, 500 people, or 10,000 people, you can import and send without rows being dropped.

### 2. Personalized Names (`{name}`, `{company}`, etc.)
Nobody likes receiving cold generic spam. With tags, your message can say:
> *"Hello {name}, your order from {company} is ready!"*

The extension automatically replaces `{name}` with "John", "Sarah", etc., for each recipient.

### 3. Send PDF Documents & Photos
* **PDFs & Invoices**: Attach brochures, contracts, receipts, or catalogs directly. The extension automatically sends PDFs as documents so they arrive crisp and uncompressed.
* **Photos & Videos**: Upload images (`.jpg`, `.png`) or short videos (`.mp4`).
* **Large Files via Link**: If you have a large file (>7MB), paste a Google Drive or cloud link in the **Paste Link** tab to send files up to 100MB.

### 4. Full Tab Mode & Side Panel
* **Side Panel**: Kept docked on the side while you look at your WhatsApp chat.
* **Full Tab (`⤢ Full Tab`)**: Click the button at the top to expand the extension into a big, comfortable dashboard. When you open Full Tab, the side panel automatically closes so you only have one clean screen.

### 5. Dark Mode & Light Mode (`🌙 / ☀️`)
Work comfortably day or night. Click the theme button at the top to toggle between dark and light themes.

### 6. Send Test Button
Never guess how your message will look. Test your text and PDF on your own phone first with one click before sending to your whole list.

### 7. History (Last 5 Days)
Keeps a clean record of who received your message and when. If a number was wrong or disconnected, you can see the error here.

## 🛡️ Safety Settings (Why Your Account Stays Safe)

WhatsApp blocks automated bots that blast 100 messages in 1 second at robotic speeds.  
This tool protects your account by acting like a **real human sitting at a keyboard**:

| Safety Guardrail | What It Means in Simple Words |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Min Delay (15s)** | The tool will always wait **at least 15 seconds** before sending the next message. |
| **Max Delay (45s)** | The upper waiting limit. The tool randomly chooses a pause between 15 and 45 seconds for each message so the timing is never identical. |
| **Hourly Batch Cap (300)** | The maximum messages sent in 1 hour. If you have 500 contacts, it will safely send 300, pause until the hour resets, and then continue. This prevents sudden volume spikes. |
| **Delay Variance / Jitter (20%)** | Adds natural human variation. Instead of waiting exactly 20.0 seconds every time (which looks like a robot), it varies the wait time (e.g., 18s, 24s, 21s, 29s) like a real person taking sips of coffee. |
| **Human Typing Simulation** | The extension doesn't instantly paste the message in one flash. It simulates natural human keystrokes with 30ms–90ms between letters. |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Do I need to keep WhatsApp Web open?
Yes. WhatsApp Web must be open in your browser tab because the extension sends messages directly through your browser.

#### Can I send PDFs and documents?
Yes! Click **Upload File**, select your PDF, and it will be delivered directly into each chat as an official document.

#### Is my data safe?
Yes, 100%. Everything runs locally inside your own browser. Your contact numbers, messages, and files are never stored on any external server or cloud database.
